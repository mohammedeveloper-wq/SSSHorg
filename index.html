A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
Start here.

New to simulation or to SSSH? Here are a few things people often ask.

Who can take part in SSSH courses?+

Our learning is for people involved in healthcare and healthcare education. Course descriptions will help you find the right fit for your role and experience.

Do I need previous simulation experience?+

Not at all. We welcome people who are completely new to simulation as well as experienced practitioners looking to develop their skills.

Where do courses take place?+

SSSH is a local society. Course locations and delivery details will be shared with each course announcement.

How can I hear about new courses?+

Use the contact form below to let us know what you are interested in, and we can share information as it becomes available.
